In these examples (from plot_beats.py), the normal (N) beat falls quickly from its starting value, stays low at about 0.1–0.2, and then shows a thin, tall spike at around 650 ms. The S and F beats have a similar sharp fall and thin spike, but S has a higher middle section with a bump near 0.55, while F has two small bumps and a much earlier spike at around 530 ms. The V and Q beats look most different from N because they stay high in the middle, at about 0.5, and Q also has a wide dome near 0.9 and the longest signal before the zero padding begins.
